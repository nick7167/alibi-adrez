## Executive recommendation

Do not ship under an App Store title led by `AHA`. Use a distinctive Danish
public title, while leaving the current in-game AHA identity untouched until the
user approves a rename.

Recommended direction:

1. `Svarspor: Gæt dine venner` — recommended
2. `Bag svaret: Gæt dine venner` — backup
3. `Vennernes svar: Gæt hvem` — backup

All three fit Apple's current 30-character title ceiling. `Svarspor` has the best
balance of distinctiveness, brevity, game relevance, and room for descriptive
keywords. The exact Danish App Store searches performed for all three returned no
indexed exact-title match, and Punktum dk WHOIS returned no entry for
`svarspor.dk`, `bagsvaret.dk`, or `vennernessvar.dk` at check time. This is only
an availability signal, not a reservation.

## Why AHA is high risk

- AhaGuess is an active iPhone/iPad party guessing game (Apple ID 6801897896)
  and an active Google Play game. Its current positioning explicitly uses an
  “Aha!” discovery moment and party guessing.
- Aha World is active in the Danish App Store with thousands of ratings and broad
  game/education visibility.
- `AHA WORLD` has a reported active EU registration covering relevant software,
  games, and entertainment classes. The official EUIPO/TMview record must still
  be reviewed directly before any legal conclusion.
- Other AHA marks exist in software and unrelated categories. A three-letter,
  exclamatory term is weakly distinctive and expensive to defend in search.
- Apple requires a unique app name and forbids misleading/copycat names or use of
  protected third-party terms in metadata.

Adding `Hvem skrev svaret?` after AHA helps App Store name availability but does
not remove the crowded-brand problem or the close AhaGuess party-game association.

### Category recommendation

- **Primary:** Games — Trivia
- **Secondary:** Entertainment

The product is unambiguously a scored game, so Games is a more truthful primary
placement than Entertainment. Trivia is imperfect—players are not answering
factual questions—but current close friend-guessing competitors use it, and the
core action is identifying the person behind an answer. Entertainment is the
best secondary browse surface for Danish party-game shoppers. Revisit this after
real category/download data exists; do not switch around launch.

## Search coverage and remaining gate

Completed:

- Danish App Store exact/current competitor searches
- Web search for exact candidate names and close party-game uses
- Direct `.dk` WHOIS checks through Punktum dk
- EUIPO/TMview, WIPO, and Danish Patent and Trademark Office source discovery
- Relevant Nice-class focus: downloadable game software (9), games (28), and
  entertainment/game services (41)

Still required before the final public name is approved:

- Run exact and fuzzy candidate searches interactively in PVSonline, TMview, and
  WIPO Global Brand Database for classes 9, 28, and 41. PVSonline presented a
  reCAPTCHA, and WIPO's terms prohibit automated querying, so these checks were
  not bypassed.
- Inspect live status, owners, goods/services, territories, and similarity—not
  only exact word matches.
- Verify the chosen title inside App Store Connect at record-creation time.
- Check/claim the final social handles and register the chosen domain; indexed
  search alone cannot reserve them.
- Consider a Danish/EU trademark professional if the product will receive
  meaningful marketing spend or broader EU distribution.
